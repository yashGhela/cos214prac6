Detailed Description

A workflow that groups other workflows and work items.

Publicly derived from WorkFlowComponent and Aggregate. It acts as the composite in the WorkFlow Composite structure and as the concrete aggregate that can create an iterator over its children.

Note
A WorkFlow owns its children and deletes them in its destructor.
